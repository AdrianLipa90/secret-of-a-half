\section{Exact half-axis geometry}

The conjugate-affine involution
\[
 K(s)=1-\overline{s}
\]
has fixed set $\Re s=1/2$. The projective coordinate
\[
 \Omega(s)=\frac{s}{1-s}
\]
sends the critical line exactly to $|\Omega|=1$. In the centered coordinate $z=s-1/2$, the completed xi function factors uniquely as
\[
 \boxed{\xi\!\left(\frac12+z\right)=F(z^2)}.
\]
The quotient coordinate $w=z^2$ sends the critical line to the non-positive real axis. Since every Taylor coefficient of $F$ is positive, $F(x)>0$ for $x\ge0$. Consequently
\[
 \boxed{\mathrm{RH}\iff\text{every zero of }F\text{ is real}.}
\]
This is SOH-G003 OPEN: an exact RH-equivalent reformulation, not a proof.

\section{Kernel and coefficient structure}

The exact positive Riemann kernel satisfies
\[
 \xi\!\left(\frac12+z\right)=\int_0^\infty\Phi(y)\cosh(zy)\,dy.
\]
SOH-G004 proves strict log-concavity of the compactified kernel weight, while the prior strict log-concavity theorem for the classical Xi kernel is attributed to Gershon. SOH-G005 proves PF$_2$ for the Taylor coefficients of $F$. SOH-G006 reduces a solid PF$_3$ Toeplitz minor to an exact ratio-curvature margin. Thus
\[
 \boxed{\mathrm{PF}_2\ \text{PROVED}},\qquad
 \boxed{\mathrm{PF}_3\ \text{OPEN}},\qquad
 \boxed{\mathrm{PF}_\infty\ \text{OPEN}}.
\]

SOH-G021 proves that the complete abstract G020 package does not imply PF$_3$: its exact counterexample has
\[
 M=-\frac{1271}{2500}<0.
\]
SOH-G022 returns to the actual G006 obstruction and proves
\[
 \boxed{
 M=(1-v)^3+v(1-w)\bigl[1-v(2-u)\bigr]+v(1-v)(w-v).
 }
\]
Therefore
\[
 0<v<1,\qquad v\le w\le1,\qquad 1-v(2-u)\ge0
\]
imply $M\ge(1-v)^3>0$ for that solid minor. The required inequalities are not proved globally for the actual Riemann sequence.

SOH-G023 introduces

and proves the exact normal form

 \boxed{

 F(R_\star)=2F(0)

\section{SOH-G024 branch candidate: direct Jensen--Wiener frontier}

SOH-G024 is not part of the canonical ledger on this branch. It starts from the even full-line kernel
\[
 K(t)=\frac12\Phi(|t|)
\]
and the centered correlation
\[
 C(u)=\int_{\mathbb R}r^2K(u+r)K(u-r)\,dr.
\]
For the Dimitrov--Xu order-two kernel, the exact substitution $t=2u$, $s=u-r$ gives
\[
 \nu_2(2u)=4C(u).
\]
With
\[
 D_y(u)=\cosh(2yu)C(u),
\]
their RH-equivalent translation-density criterion becomes, by Wiener's theorem, strict positivity of $\widehat D_y$ for every real frequency and every $0<|y|<1/2$.

The branch records the sufficient open target
\[
 H_y(q)=D_y(\sqrt q),\qquad
 (-1)^mH_y^{(m)}(q)\ge0
\]
for every $m\ge0$, $q\ge0$, and $0<|y|<1/2$. If proved, Bernstein's theorem would represent $D_y$ as a positive Gaussian mixture and force $\widehat D_y>0$, hence RH. Complete monotonicity is not proved.

The branch also defines the distinct internal tilt
\[
 J_y(u)=\int_{\mathbb R}r^2\cosh(2yr)K(u+r)K(u-r)\,dr,
\]
which carries the complex Jensen/Laguerre functional. The proof firewall is
\[
 \boxed{D_y\ne J_y\text{ in general for }y\ne0},\qquad
 \boxed{D_0=J_0=C}.
\]
Finite derivative diagnostics do not promote this candidate.
