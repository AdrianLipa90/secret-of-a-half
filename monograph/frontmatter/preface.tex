The purpose of this text is to keep distinct three questions that are easily conflated:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Why is $1/2$ the centre of the functional symmetry of the completed zeta function?
  \item Why does the same half recur in binary balance, equal-amplitude cancellation, projective self-duality, and spinorial phase closure?
  \item What additional analytic mechanism, if any, forces every non-trivial zeta zero onto that axis?
\end{enumerate}
The first two questions admit exact answers in the declared mathematical representations. The third contains the Riemann-Hypothesis difficulty and remains open \citep{clay2026,bombieri2006}.

Version 0.10 Mainline Integration applies a typed claim discipline throughout the monograph:
\begin{description}
  \item[Exact or standard.] A complete derivation is supplied from stated definitions, or a classical theorem is invoked with assumptions and citation.
  \item[Conditional.] The conclusion follows rigorously once a named unproved hypothesis is granted.
  \item[Computer-assisted.] A universal statement depends on a reproducible interval or symbolic certificate with an explicit trust boundary.
  \item[Model/software.] A TIR/PhaseNav or information-spinor construction is used only inside its declared layer and cannot promote a mathematical claim by itself.
  \item[Finite diagnostic.] Numerical sampling or regression evidence with a stated finite scope; never a universal proof.
  \item[OPEN.] A missing bridge or sign condition that is not promoted automatically.
\end{description}
Whenever prose adds an interpretive reading rather than a mathematical implication, it is marked \textbf{INTERPRETACJA}. This is a semantic firewall, not a proof status.

The G024 line distinguishes two different $y$-tilts. The external Dimitrov--Xu family
\[
D_y(u)=\cosh(2yu)C(u)
\]
is tied directly to the Wiener density criterion \citep{dimitrovxu2016,wiener1932tauberian}. The internal family $J_y$ carries a distinct complex Jensen/Laguerre functional. They coincide at $y=0$ but are not identified for $y\ne0$.

The complete-monotonicity route is now partially closed rather than merely proposed. For
\[
H_y(q)=D_y(\sqrt q),
\]
G024 proves $H_y'(q)<0$ and $H_y''(q)>0$ globally throughout $0<|y|<1/2$. The second-order compact closure depends explicitly on SOH-G024-Q, an outward \texttt{mpmath.iv} interval certificate combined with an analytic infinite-theta-tail enclosure and an analytic large-$t$ proof. This follows validated-numerics methodology rather than finite-grid inference \citep{moore2009interval}. The third-order sign and all orders $m\ge3$ remain open.

The proof firewall is therefore multi-route but consistent. SOH-C001 remains open for the normalized information-spinor state-map programme. SOH-C005 remains the global Weil/Li positivity frontier. SOH-G003 real-rootedness remains open; actual PF$_3$ and PF$_\infty$ remain open. The negative-inversion root-invariance route is closed negatively by SOH-G018. G024 has global first- and second-order complete monotonicity but not full complete monotonicity or strict external Fourier positivity. None of these statements proves RH. The Riemann Hypothesis remains OPEN in this monograph.
