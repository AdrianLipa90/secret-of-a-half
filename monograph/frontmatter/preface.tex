The purpose of this text is to keep distinct three questions that are easily conflated:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Why is $1/2$ the centre of the functional symmetry of the completed zeta function?
  \item Why does the same half recur in binary balance, equal-amplitude cancellation, projective self-duality, and spinorial phase closure?
  \item What additional analytic mechanism, if any, forces every non-trivial zeta zero onto that axis?
\end{enumerate}
The first two questions admit exact answers in the declared mathematical representations. The third contains the Riemann-Hypothesis difficulty and remains open \citep{clay2026,bombieri2006}.

Version 0.9 Integrated Canon V3 applies a typed claim discipline throughout the monograph:
\begin{description}
  \item[Exact or standard.] A complete derivation is supplied from stated definitions, or a classical theorem is invoked with its assumptions and citation.
  \item[Conditional.] The conclusion follows rigorously once a named unproved hypothesis is granted.
  \item[Model.] A TIR/PhaseNav or information-spinor assignment used only inside an explicitly declared modelling layer.
  \item[Open.] A missing bridge that is never promoted automatically.
  \item[Numerical.] A finite computation, regression check, or certificate with a stated finite scope.
\end{description}
Whenever the prose adds an interpretive reading rather than a mathematical implication, it is explicitly marked \textbf{INTERPRETACJA}. This label is not a proof status; it is a semantic firewall separating interpretation from theorem content.

The claim map is cumulative. SOH-L001--SOH-L032 retain the stable foundational and Li/Weil meanings recorded in \cref{app:claims}. The later SOH-G line records the quotient, kernel, total-positivity, Uroboros, negative-inversion, zero-exclusion, coefficient-normalization, and PF$_3$ programme through SOH-G022. Historical identifiers that collided during development remain provenance aliases only; they do not redefine the active claims.

Readers interested in the elementary mathematical core may begin with \cref{chap:symmetry,chap:entropy,chap:cancellation,chap:eta}. The initial information-spinor ansatz and its conditional theorem are developed in Chapters~8--15. The native arithmetic programme occupies \cref{chap:phasenav-native,ch:hermite-ladder,ch:prime-tail-certificate,ch:adaptive-cutoff}. The identity, holonomy, typed-solver, and Li/Weil integration follows in Chapters~22--25. The global quotient and positive-kernel line begins in Chapter~26; Chapters~29--31 contain the log-concavity, PF$_2$, and PF$_3$ frontier. Chapters~32--43 separate exact Uroboros/projective/spinorial geometry from the negative-inversion spectral no-go, culminating in the complete exclusion of negative-inversion zero pairing. Chapters~44--45 establish the coefficient-majorant positive-real-part disk and the canonical half-mass PF$_2$ law. Chapter~46 synthesizes the audited canon. Chapter~47 proves that the complete abstract G020 half-mass/PF$_2$/envelope package is insufficient by itself to imply PF$_3$. Chapter~48 then returns to the exact G006 margin and derives a one-step curvature barrier that is sufficient for positivity of an individual solid order-three minor. Neither result decides PF$_3$ for the actual quotient coefficients.

The proof firewall is therefore multi-branch but consistent. SOH-C001 remains open for the specific normalized information-spinor state-map programme. SOH-C005 remains the open global Weil/Li positivity frontier. In the quotient line, SOH-G003 real-rootedness remains open; PF$_3$ for the actual quotient coefficients and PF$_\infty$ remain open. SOH-G021 closes only the abstract implication from the G020 structural package to PF$_3$. SOH-G022 proves a local sufficient condition for the G006 solid margin, while the required ratio-curvature inequalities and the upgrade from solid minors to full PF$_3$ remain open. The negative-inversion root-invariance route, by contrast, is closed negatively by SOH-G018. None of these distinctions changes the final status: the Riemann Hypothesis remains OPEN in this monograph.
