\chapter{A One-Step Curvature Barrier for the Solid PF3 Margin}
\label{chap:g022-pf3-curvature-barrier}

\status{Exact sufficient-condition theorem for the G006 solid PF3 margin; global PF3 remains open.}

\section{Purpose}

SOH-G006 reduced the sign of the solid order-three Toeplitz minor to an exact
three-ratio expression. SOH-G021 then proved that the abstract structural
package supplied by the canonical half-mass PF2 law is insufficient, by
itself, to imply PF3. The present chapter isolates a stronger local condition
that directly controls the G006 obstruction.

No claim is made here that the Riemann coefficient sequence satisfies the new
condition at every index. The result is a sufficient-condition theorem plus a
finite regression diagnostic.

\section{The G006 margin}

Let
\[
 F(w)=\sum_{k\ge0}a_k w^k,
 \qquad a_k>0,
\]
and define
\[
 r_k=\frac{a_k}{a_{k-1}},
 \qquad
 q_k=\frac{r_{k+1}}{r_k}.
\]
For a solid G006 minor, write
\[
 u=q_{k-1},\qquad v=q_k,\qquad w=q_{k+1}.
\]
The exact ratio-curvature margin is
\[
 M=(1-v)^2-v^2(1-u)(1-w).
\]
The corresponding solid Toeplitz determinant has the same sign as \(M\).

\section{Exact decomposition}

\begin{theorem}[SOH-G022 curvature-barrier decomposition]
The G006 solid-minor margin satisfies the polynomial identity
\[
 \boxed{
 M=(1-v)^3
 +v(1-w)\bigl[1-v(2-u)\bigr]
 +v(1-v)(w-v).
 }
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Expand the right-hand side and collect the coefficients of the monomials in
\(u,v,w\). The result is exactly
\[
 (1-v)^2-v^2(1-u)(1-w).
\]
\end{proof}

Define
\[
 B_k:=1-v(2-u),
 \qquad
 G_k:=w-v.
\]
Then the same identity is
\[
 M=(1-v)^3+v(1-w)B_k+v(1-v)G_k.
\]

\section{A local sufficient condition}

\begin{corollary}[One-step barrier certificate]
If
\[
 0<v<1,
 \qquad
 v\le w\le1,
 \qquad
 1-v(2-u)\ge0,
\]
then
\[
 \boxed{M\ge(1-v)^3>0.}
\]
Hence the corresponding solid G006 order-three Toeplitz minor is strictly
positive.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Under the stated hypotheses, each of the three terms in the G022
decomposition is non-negative, while \((1-v)^3\) is strictly positive.
\end{proof}

For \(v>0\), the barrier has the equivalent forms
\[
 1-u\le\frac{1-v}{v},
 \qquad
 v\le\frac{1}{2-u}.
\]
Writing the PF2 curvature deficits as
\[
 d_j:=1-q_j,
\]
the barrier becomes
\[
 \boxed{
 d_k\ge\frac{d_{k-1}}{1+d_{k-1}}.
 }
\]
Thus the local decrease of the deficit is quantitatively constrained rather
than merely monotone.

\section{Normalized-moment formulation}

For the Riemann-kernel coefficient sequence, write
\[
 R(p)=\Gamma(p+1)^{-1}\int_0^\infty y^p\Phi(y)\,dy,
 \qquad
 L(p)=\log R(p),
\]
so that \(a_k=R(2k)\). Define
\[
 \delta_k=L(2k+2)-2L(2k)+L(2k-2).
\]
Then
\[
 q_k=e^{\delta_k}.
\]
The two G022 local obligations become
\[
 \boxed{\delta_{k+1}\ge\delta_k}
\]
and
\[
 \boxed{
 e^{\delta_k}\bigl(2-e^{\delta_{k-1}}\bigr)\le1.
 }
\]
Equivalently,
\[
 \boxed{
 \delta_k\le-\log\!\bigl(2-e^{\delta_{k-1}}\bigr).
 }
\]
SOH-G005 supplies only \(\delta_k\le0\). Therefore G022 isolates strictly
stronger, kernel-specific targets rather than repackaging PF2.

\section{Compatibility with the G021 no-go}

The exact G021 counterexample uses
\[
 u=\frac15,
 \qquad
 v=\frac9{10},
 \qquad
 w=\frac15.
\]
For this triple,
\[
 1-v(2-u)=-\frac{31}{50}<0,
 \qquad
 w-v=-\frac7{10}<0.
\]
Thus the G021 construction violates both nontrivial G022 hypotheses. The two
results are therefore logically compatible: G021 blocks the weaker structural
package, whereas G022 identifies additional local structure that would be
sufficient for the solid minor.

\section{Finite Riemann diagnostic}

The accompanying deterministic receipt recomputes a finite set of
positive-kernel moments and records \(u,v,w\), the barrier, the forward order
gap, the cubic floor, and the exact decomposition residual. Finite success is
recorded only as a regression fact and is not promoted to a theorem about all
indices.

\section{Open obligations}

The exact next obligations are:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item prove \(q_{k+1}\ge q_k\) for every relevant Riemann coefficient index;
  \item prove \(q_k(2-q_{k-1})\le1\) for every such index;
  \item only then conclude positivity of all solid G006 order-three minors;
  \item supply a separate argument upgrading solid-minor control to all
        order-three Toeplitz minors before claiming PF3.
\end{enumerate}
