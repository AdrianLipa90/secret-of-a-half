\section{Exact half-axis geometry}

The affine symmetry data select the half without proving the zero-location theorem. The conjugate-affine involution
\[
 K(s)=1-\overline{s}
\]
has fixed set $\Re s=1/2$. The projective coordinate
\[
 \Omega(s)=\frac{s}{1-s}
\]
sends the critical line exactly to $|\Omega|=1$, while radial compactification sends that circle to the self-dual layer $q=1/2$. In the centered coordinate $z=s-1/2$, the functional reflection is $z\mapsto-z$, so the completed xi function factors uniquely as
\[
 \boxed{\xi\!\left(\frac12+z\right)=F(z^2)}.
\]
The quotient coordinate $w=z^2$ sends the critical line to the non-positive real axis. Because every Taylor coefficient of $F$ is positive, $F(x)>0$ for $x\ge0$. Consequently
\[
 \boxed{\mathrm{RH}\iff\text{every zero of }F\text{ is real}.}
\]
This is the SOH-G003 frontier and remains an exact reformulation, not a solution.

\section{Proved analytic structure of the quotient}

The monograph derives the exact positive half-line Riemann kernel
\[
 \xi\!\left(\frac12+z\right)=\int_0^\infty\Phi(y)\cosh(zy)\,dy,

and its compactified positive weight $W$. Strict log-concavity of $W$ is proved in SOH-G004. Strict log-concavity of the classical Xi kernel $\Phi$ is independently established in the prior 2026 work of Avi Gershon and is explicitly attributed where used.

The normalized moments of $\Phi$ give the coefficients
\[
 F(w)=\sum_{n\ge0}a_nw^n,
 \qquad a_n>0.
\]
SOH-G005 proves that $(a_n)$ is PF$_2$. SOH-G006 reduces the first solid PF$_3$ Toeplitz minor to an exact ratio-curvature margin. What is not proved is global non-negativity of that margin. Therefore
\[
 \boxed{\mathrm{PF}_2\ \text{PROVED}},
 \qquad
 \boxed{\mathrm{PF}_3\ \text{OPEN}},
 \qquad
 \boxed{\mathrm{PF}_\infty\ \text{OPEN}}.
\]
No implication from PF$_2$ to real-rootedness is assumed.

SOH-G021 sharpens the boundary without deciding the actual PF$_3$ question. Chapter~47 constructs an exact positive half-mass PF$_2$ probability law satisfying the same strict mass decay and normalized envelope proved in SOH-G020, but with G006 margin

 \boxed{M=-\frac{1271}{2500}<0.}

Thus the abstract G020 structural package does not imply PF$_3$. This is a no-go for a proof shortcut, not a negative PF$_3$ result for the actual coefficients of $F$.

SOH-G022 then returns to the exact G006 obstruction. For consecutive ratio-curvatures $u,v,w$, it proves the identity
\[
 \boxed{
 M=(1-v)^3+v(1-w)\bigl[1-v(2-u)\bigr]+v(1-v)(w-v).

Consequently, the local conditions
\[
 0<v<1,\qquad v\le w\le1,\qquad 1-v(2-u)\ge0

\[
 \boxed{M\ge(1-v)^3>0.}
\]
This is an exact sufficient condition for the corresponding \emph{solid} order-three minor. The two local ratio-curvature inequalities are not proved globally for the actual Riemann coefficient sequence, and solid-minor positivity is not identified with full PF$_3$ without an additional theorem.

\section{Negative inversion: exact geometry, closed spectral route}

The canonical negative inverse is an exact coordinate operator. Riemann reflection and the Euler half-turn generate the projective Klein four group, and the corresponding Pauli lifts generate $Q_8$ with projective quotient $Q_8/\{\pm I\}\cong V_4$. These algebraic statements remain valid.

Their spectral interpretation is resolved separately. SOH-G014--G017 progressively restrict possible zero pairing, and SOH-G018 proves the stronger final result
\[
 \boxed{P_J=\varnothing},
 \qquad
 \boxed{P_N=\varnothing}.
\]
Equivalently, the canonical negative inversion maps no zero of $F$ to another zero of $F$ and no zero of $\xi$ to another zero of $\xi$.

Thus the negative-inversion line has two distinct current statuses:
\begin{itemize}
 \item its projective and spinorial operator geometry is exact and retained;
 \item root-set invariance under that operator is not an open conjecture but a proved no-go.
\end{itemize}
This distinction prevents a valid coordinate symmetry from being promoted into a false spectral symmetry.

\section{Coefficient-majorant and half-mass structure}

SOH-G019 defines the unique positive threshold $R_\star$ by
\[
 F(R_\star)=2F(0)
\]
and proves
\[
 \Re F(w)>0\qquad(|w|\le R_\star).
\]
Hence the disk is zero-free. The normalized function
\[
 H(\zeta)=\frac{F(R_\star\zeta)}{F(0)}
\]
has $H(0)=1$ and positive real part on the unit disk, placing it in the classical Carath\'eodory class.

SOH-G020 then defines
\[
 \pi_n=\frac{a_nR_\star^n}{2F(0)}
\]
and proves
\[
 \pi_n>0,
 \qquad
 \sum_{n\ge0}\pi_n=1,
 \qquad
 \pi_0=\sum_{n\ge1}\pi_n=\frac12.
\]
The sequence remains PF$_2$, is strictly decreasing, and satisfies the sharpened coefficient bound
\[
 a_n<\frac{F(0)}{nR_\star^n}
 \qquad(n\ge1).
\]
These results provide exact coefficient-level structure but do not imply PF$_3$, PF$_\infty$, or real-rootedness. SOH-G021 proves the first of those non-implications constructively at the level of the complete abstract G020 package, while SOH-G022 identifies additional local structure that would be sufficient for the G006 solid margin.

\section{Independent open branches}

The monograph now contains several precisely separated open frontiers.

\begin{description}
 \item[SOH-C001.] The normalized information-spinor state-map construction remains open. Later scalar kernels and quotient functions do not retroactively instantiate its channel-metric hypotheses.
 \item[SOH-C005.] Full admissible Weil positivity remains open and is RH-equivalent under the standard criterion. It cannot be assumed as an auxiliary lemma.
 \item[SOH-G003.] Real-rootedness of the quotient entire function $F$ remains open and is RH-equivalent after the proved coefficient positivity.
 \item[PF$_3$/PF$_\infty$.] The coefficient hierarchy is proved only through PF$_2$. G021 proves that the G020 half-mass/PF$_2$/envelope package cannot determine the G006 margin. G022 gives a local sufficient condition for a solid minor, but the required inequalities $q_{k+1}\ge q_k$ and $q_k(2-q_{k-1})\le1$ are not proved globally for the actual coefficients, and a separate upgrade from solid minors to all order-three Toeplitz minors is still required.
\end{description}

These branches may constrain one another, but no equivalence between their unproved intermediate mechanisms is asserted unless explicitly proved.

Several tempting routes are no longer admissible proof strategies because the monograph has either proved their insufficiency or directly falsified them:

 \item functional symmetry alone does not force pointwise zero fixation;

 \item canonical negative inversion does not permute the xi or $F$ zero sets; SOH-G018 proves complete absence of such pairing.
