\section{Exact half-axis, projective, and quotient geometry}
The conjugate-affine involution $K(s)=1-\bar s$ has fixed set $\Re s=1/2$. With
\[
u=\Omega(s)=\frac{s}{1-s},
\]
the critical line is exactly $|u|=1$. The functional reflection $s\mapsto1-s$ becomes $u\mapsto1/u$, while complex conjugation becomes $u\mapsto\bar u$. Hence
\[
\boxed{\frac1u=\bar u\iff |u|=1\iff\Re s=\frac12.}
\]
For the transformed function $X(u)=\xi(u/(1+u))$,
\[
\boxed{\mathrm{RH}\iff \forall u\in Z_X:\ u^{-1}=\bar u.}
\]
The orbit-separating defect
\[
\Delta_{\mathrm{RC}}(u)=\left|u^{-1}-\bar u\right|^2
=\left(|u|-|u|^{-1}\right)^2
\]
vanishes exactly on the same locus. The implication $X(u)=0\Rightarrow\Delta_{\mathrm{RC}}(u)=0$ remains RH-equivalent and OPEN.

In the centred coordinate $z=s-1/2$,
\[
\boxed{\xi\!\left(\frac12+z\right)=F(z^2)}.
\]
The square quotient sends the critical line to the negative real axis, and positivity of the Taylor coefficients excludes non-negative real roots. Therefore
\[
\boxed{\mathrm{RH}\iff\text{every zero of }F\text{ is real}.}
\]
This is SOH-G003 OPEN.
