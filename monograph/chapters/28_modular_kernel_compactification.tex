\chapter{Modular Compactification of the Positive Riemann Kernel}
\label{chap:modular-kernel-compactification}

\status{Exact change of variables and reciprocal-kernel geometry; SOH-G003 remains open.}

\section{Purpose}

The previous chapter derives the exact positive Riemann kernel
\[
\xi\!\left(\frac12+z\right)=\int_0^\infty \Phi(y)\cosh(zy)\,dy,
\qquad \Phi(y)>0.
\]
This chapter places that same integral directly in the reciprocal geometry used by the global inverse-boundary programme.  No new zero-location assumption is introduced.

\section{Mellin reciprocal coordinate}

Set
\[
x=e^{2y},\qquad x\ge1.
\]
Then
\[
dy=\frac{dx}{2x},
\qquad
\cosh(zy)=\frac12\left(x^{z/2}+x^{-z/2}\right).
\]
Using the explicit kernel from \cref{chap:positive-riemann-kernel}, one obtains
\begin{equation}
\boxed{
\xi\!\left(\frac12+z\right)
=\int_1^\infty D(x)
\left(x^{z/2}+x^{-z/2}\right)\,dx
}
\label{eq:modular-mellin-pair}
\end{equation}
with
\begin{equation}
\boxed{
D(x)=\sum_{n\ge1}\pi n^2x^{1/4}
\left(2\pi n^2x-3\right)e^{-\pi n^2x}.
}
\label{eq:modular-density}
\end{equation}
For $x\ge1$, every summand in \cref{eq:modular-density} is positive because
\[
2\pi n^2x-3\ge2\pi-3>0.
\]
Hence
\[
D(x)>0.
\]

The two Mellin channels in \cref{eq:modular-mellin-pair} are exchanged by
\[
x\longmapsto x^{-1}.
\]
Thus $x\ge1$ is a quotient representative of the reciprocal positive line rather than a loss of one of the two channels.

\section{Compactified signed radius}

Define
\begin{equation}
\boxed{
\eta=\frac{x-1}{x+1}.
}
\label{eq:kernel-eta}
\end{equation}
Then
\[
x=\frac{1+\eta}{1-\eta},
\qquad
\eta=\tanh y,
\]
and on the full positive line
\begin{equation}
\boxed{
x\mapsto x^{-1}
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\eta\mapsto-\eta.
}
\label{eq:kernel-inversion-eta}
\end{equation}
The distinguished locations are
\[
x\to0^+\mapsto\eta\to-1,
\qquad
x=1\mapsto\eta=0,
\qquad
x\to\infty\mapsto\eta\to1.
\]
Therefore the Mellin variable carries the same signed reciprocal compactification as the inverse-boundary coordinate of \cref{ch:compactified-inverse-boundary}: zero and infinity occupy opposite compactified boundaries, while the reciprocal fixed point occupies the centre.

\section{Exact compactified kernel representation}

Since
\[
\frac{dx}{d\eta}=\frac{2}{(1-\eta)^2},
\qquad
\frac12\log x=\operatorname{artanh}\eta,
\]
\cref{eq:modular-mellin-pair} becomes
\begin{equation}
\boxed{
\xi\!\left(\frac12+z\right)
=
\int_0^1 W(\eta)
\cosh\!\left(z\operatorname{artanh}\eta\right)\,d\eta,
}
\label{eq:compactified-riemann-kernel}
\end{equation}
where
\begin{equation}
\boxed{
W(\eta)
=
\frac{4D\!\left((1+\eta)/(1-\eta)\right)}{(1-\eta)^2}>0.
}
\label{eq:compactified-weight}
\end{equation}
This is an exact change of variables.  In particular, the compactification does not replace the analytic real-rootedness problem by a numerical approximation.

\section{Classical route audit}

De Bruijn's study of trigonometric integrals gives sufficient real-zero mechanisms for several classes of kernels, including factors built from $\exp(-\lambda\cosh t)$ and functions of $\cosh t$ satisfying strong zero-location hypotheses.  In his discussion of the Riemann kernel, however, de Bruijn explicitly records the obstruction to simply approximating the exact kernel by the relevant analytic universal-factor class: the required global analytic continuation is absent.  Therefore those sufficient theorems cannot be invoked for the exact Riemann kernel without an additional argument establishing their hypotheses.

This is a proof-firewall statement: the classical theory is relevant, but it does not by itself close SOH-G003.

\section{What the compactification actually adds}

Writing
\[
\xi\!\left(\frac12+z\right)=F(z^2),
\]
the active target remains
\[
F(w)=0\Longrightarrow w\in\mathbb R.
\]
The new exact structure is that the positive measure generating $F$ now lives on a compact reciprocal coordinate, with
\[
\eta=0
\]
as the modular self-dual point and
\[
\eta\mapsto-\eta
\]
as the exact reciprocal involution.

\gap{A property of the exact positive weight $W(\eta)$ strong enough to imply real-rootedness of $F$ has not yet been proved.}

\begin{warning}
Neither positivity of $W$ nor reciprocal compactification alone implies SOH-G003.  The Riemann Hypothesis remains open.
\end{warning}
