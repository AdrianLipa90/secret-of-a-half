# SOH-C005 Log-Radial Operator Reduction

Define \(B(s)=\log|\Omega(s)|\) and \(V(s)=B(s)^2\). The critical line is exactly \(B=0\). In the prime-side screw/Weil representation, arithmetic support occurs at additive shifts \(\pm\log n\), so the same logarithmic radial coordinate linearizes the prime-shift structure.

A termwise Gram shortcut fails: for \(h_a(t)=(|t|-a)_+\), the associated two-point screw kernel at \(x=a/2\), \(y=3a/2\) has determinant \(-a^2/4<0\). Thus positivity of \(\Lambda(n)\) alone cannot prove SOH-C005.

The remaining target is
\[
\boxed{A_a\ge0\quad\text{for every }a>0,}
\]
equivalently non-negativity of the spectral floor \(\lambda(a)\), derived independently from prime/theta/operator structure.
