The purpose of this text is to keep distinct three questions that are easily conflated:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Why is $1/2$ the centre of the functional symmetry of the completed zeta function?
  \item Why does the same half recur in binary balance, equal-amplitude cancellation, projective self-duality, and spinorial phase closure?
  \item Why should every non-trivial zeta zero be fixed by the symmetry rather than merely paired by it?
\end{enumerate}
The first two questions admit exact answers in the declared mathematical representations. The third is the Riemann Hypothesis and remains open \citep{clay2026,bombieri2006}.

Version 0.9 applies a typed claim discipline throughout the integrated monograph:
\begin{description}
  \item[Exact or standard.] A complete derivation is supplied from stated definitions, or a classical theorem is invoked with its assumptions and citation.
  \item[Conditional.] The conclusion follows rigorously once a named unproved hypothesis is granted.
  \item[Model.] A TIR/PhaseNav assignment used only inside an explicitly declared modelling layer.
  \item[Open.] A missing bridge that is never promoted automatically.
  \item[Numerical.] A finite computation, regression check, or certificate with a stated finite scope.
\end{description}

The current canonical claim IDs SOH-L012--SOH-L032 belong to the V2 integration developed in \cref{chap:li-negative-inverse}. Earlier development snapshots used several of those numbers for Hermite, reciprocal-tail, and DHSE results. No result is discarded: the earlier statements are retained under domain-specific canonical IDs and their historical aliases are documented in \cref{app:claims}. This prevents one identifier from denoting two different propositions in the current monograph.

Readers interested in the elementary mathematical core may begin with \cref{chap:symmetry,chap:entropy,chap:cancellation,chap:eta}. The native arithmetic programme occupies \cref{chap:phasenav-native,ch:hermite-ladder,ch:prime-tail-certificate,ch:adaptive-cutoff}. The geometry-first identity, holonomy, and typed-solver integration follows in \cref{chap:identity-axis,chap:geometry-dependencies,chap:typed-solver}. The final synthesis in \cref{chap:li-negative-inverse} connects the projective half-axis map to Li's negative inverse and to the global Weil positivity frontier.

The central proof firewall is unchanged by the additional structure: exact reformulations, exact no-go lemmas, and finite positive-semidefinite receipts do not prove the global arithmetic positivity statement. SOH-C005 and the Riemann Hypothesis therefore remain explicitly OPEN.
