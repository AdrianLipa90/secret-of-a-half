\chapter{Falsification Programme and Research Milestones}
\label{chap:falsification}

\section{Principle of progress}

The project advances by closing explicit gaps, not by accumulating analogies. Each milestone below was formulated with a pass condition, a fail condition, and an output artifact. A failed candidate remains useful when its failure is recorded precisely and prevents repetition.

\section{Initial preregistered milestone baseline}

The following milestones record the state-map research plan at the stage at which Chapters~8--15 were formulated. They are retained as a provenance baseline rather than silently rewritten as if they had anticipated every later branch.

\paragraph{Milestone 1: formalize the exact core.}
Maintain fully written proofs of the entropy, cancellation, involution, eta, and asymmetric-channel results, with explicit domains and regression tests. This milestone is achieved at the monograph level.

\paragraph{Milestone 2: theta-kernel channel decomposition.}
Derive two complementary analytic vector components from the theta representation that swap under $s\mapsto1-s$, remain jointly non-zero at detector zeros, and reconstruct $\xi$ up to a non-vanishing factor. This specific two-component state construction remains OPEN, although Chapter~27 later derives a different exact positive scalar Riemann kernel.

\paragraph{Milestone 3: derive the channel metric.}
Construct a positive swap-invariant Hermitian metric in which normalized state components have weights $\sigma$ and $1-\sigma$ without imposing them by hand. This remains OPEN for SOH-C001.

\paragraph{Milestone 4: zero-readout equivalence.}
Prove that a fixed equal-gain state detector vanishes exactly when $\xi(s)$ does on the open strip, with controlled multiplicity and a non-vanishing prefactor. This remains OPEN for SOH-C001.

\paragraph{Milestone 5: positivity or self-adjointness.}
Construct a rigorous positive kernel or self-adjoint operator with all domain, closure, determinant, and arithmetic requirements needed for a zero-location theorem. The monograph now contains exact positive kernels and localized Weil operator statements, but no construction satisfying the global RH-closing determinant or full Weil-positivity requirement. SOH-C005 remains OPEN.

\paragraph{Milestone 6: independent adversarial review.}
Subject any claimed closing theorem to independent specialists in analytic number theory, spectral theory, and functional analysis. This remains a publication requirement; no RH proof is claimed here.

\section{Hard falsifiers of the information-spinor state-map route}

The state-map mechanism should be rejected in its stated form if a theorem establishes any of the following:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item no involution-covariant analytic vector decomposition can satisfy the required norm ratio in any positive swap-invariant metric;
  \item every eta- or theta-derived two-channel decomposition either vanishes jointly at zeta zeros or has unavoidable false-positive detector zeros;
  \item the required equal-gain condition contradicts the arithmetic structure of every canonical decomposition;
  \item any factorization with the stated weights is necessarily non-canonical or equivalent to preselecting the critical line.
\end{enumerate}
Such results would reject this mechanism, not the Riemann Hypothesis itself.

\section{Progress obtained after the baseline}

The later parts of this monograph materially change the research landscape while leaving the original open state-map bridge identifiable.

\begin{description}
 \item[Positive kernel.] Chapter~27 derives the exact positive Riemann kernel and its strictly positive moment representation. This closes the scalar kernel-construction step, but not the two-component state-map metric of SOH-C001.
 \item[Kernel shape.] Chapter~29 proves strict log-concavity of the compactified weight and records Gershon's independent prior proof of strict log-concavity of the classical Xi kernel. Log-concavity alone does not imply real-rootedness.
 \item[Coefficient total positivity.] Chapter~30 proves PF$_2$. Chapter~31 reduces the first PF$_3$ solid minor to an exact ratio-curvature margin. PF$_3$ remains OPEN.
 \item[Negative-inversion route.] Chapters~37--38 prove the projective $V_4$ algebra and its $Q_8$ spinor lift. Chapters~39--43 then prove that this exact operator geometry has no zero-to-zero spectral action: SOH-G018 gives $P_J=P_N=\varnothing$. Root-set invariance under negative inversion is therefore a closed, falsified proof route rather than an active conjecture.
 \item[Coefficient disk and probability law.] Chapters~44--45 prove the coefficient-majorant positive-real-part disk and the canonical half-mass PF$_2$ probability law, including strict monotone decay and a sharpened coefficient envelope.
\end{description}

\section{Current active frontier after Chapter~45}

The highest-value unresolved tasks are now more specific than the original milestone list:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item prove or falsify global non-negativity of the exact PF$_3$ ratio-curvature margin from Chapter~31;
  \item identify a kernel- or coefficient-specific zero-preserving mechanism stronger than positivity, log-concavity, and PF$_2$;
  \item test whether the canonical half-mass law of Chapter~45 yields new inequalities strong enough to advance PF$_3$ or higher total positivity without assuming real-rootedness;
  \item maintain explicit attribution boundaries, especially for the classical Xi-kernel log-concavity result of Gershon;
  \item keep SOH-C005 as the separate Weil/Li global-positivity frontier and avoid treating an RH-equivalent criterion as an independently proved lemma;
  \item do not reopen negative-inversion root invariance as a proof mechanism, because SOH-G018 has already excluded it exactly.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Publication thresholds}

The project uses explicit labels:
\begin{description}
  \item[Exploratory note.] Exact elementary lemmas plus conjectural architecture.
  \item[Research monograph.] Full derivations, no-go results, reproducible computations, and an explicit open-gap ledger. The present version has this status.
  \item[Conditional theorem paper.] A non-trivial canonical construction is achieved but one major positivity or equivalence assumption remains.
  \item[Claimed RH proof.] All bridges are proved, independently audited, and no hypothesis equivalent to RH remains hidden.
\end{description}
Moving between labels requires a documented claim-ledger update.
