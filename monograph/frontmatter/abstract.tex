\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}

This monograph studies the structural role of $1/2$ in the analytic geometry surrounding the Riemann zeta function while separating exact theorems, equivalent reformulations, computer-assisted certificates, finite diagnostics, model constructions, no-go results, and open proof obligations.

The foundational layer proves several exact selections of the half. Binary Shannon entropy is uniquely maximal at $\sigma=1/2$ with value $\ln2$; normalized equal-gain two-channel cancellation occurs exactly at equal weights and relative phase $\pi$; and the conjugate-affine involution $s\mapsto1-\bar s$ has fixed line $\Re s=1/2$. These statements establish structural coincidences in their declared representations but do not by themselves locate every non-trivial zeta zero.

The projective coordinate
\[
\Omega(s)=\frac{s}{1-s}
\]
maps the critical line exactly to the unit circle. Functional reflection $s\mapsto1-s$ becomes the holomorphic reciprocal action $u\mapsto1/u$, while complex conjugation becomes $u\mapsto\bar u$. Their coincidence is exact:
\[
\frac1u=\bar u
\iff |u|=1
\iff \Re s=\frac12.
\]
Consequently RH is equivalent to reciprocal--conjugation orbit collapse on every transformed xi zero. The orbit-separating defect
\[
\Delta_{\mathrm{RC}}(u)=\left|\frac1u-\bar u\right|^2
=\left(|u|-|u|^{-1}\right)^2
\]
vanishes exactly on the critical line. This is an exact RH-equivalent reformulation; the implication from zerohood to vanishing of the defect remains OPEN.

The centred quotient
\[
\xi\!\left(\frac12+z\right)=F(z^2)
\]
converts RH into real-rootedness of the entire function $F$. The monograph derives the positive Riemann kernel, proves the declared log-concavity and PF$_2$ coefficient results, develops exact PF$_3$ reductions, and records the proved no-go that the current half-mass/PF$_2$ package does not imply PF$_3$. SOH-G003 real-rootedness, actual PF$_3$, and PF$_\infty$ remain OPEN.

The Li/Weil line places Li's coordinate $1-1/s=-1/\Omega(s)$, localized Weil operators, a Hermite dense core, and certified prime-tail controls in one audited framework. The negative inversion $-1/u$ is rigorously separated from the functional reciprocal symmetry $1/u$: the former has exact projective/spinorial geometry but is excluded as a zero-to-zero spectral shortcut. Full admissible Weil positivity SOH-C005 remains an RH-equivalent OPEN frontier.

The integrated SOH-G024 Jensen--Wiener line studies
\[
K(t)=\frac12\Phi(|t|),\qquad
C(u)=\int_{\mathbb R}r^2K(u+r)K(u-r)\,dr,
\qquad
D_y(u)=\cosh(2yu)C(u),
\]
and $H_y(q)=D_y(\sqrt q)$. The Dimitrov--Xu/Wiener criterion reduces RH to strict positivity of $\widehat D_y$ for every real frequency and $0<|y|<1/2$. The monograph records the global results
\[
H_y'(q)<0,\qquad H_y''(q)>0,
\]
where the second-order compact closure has an explicit outward interval-arithmetic dependency.

SOH-G024-T is a publication-critical update.

The Riemann kernel obeys a double-exponential tail, and the proved strong convexity $-(\log K)''>17$ yields, for every fixed admissible $y$,
\[
\forall T>0:\qquad e^{Tq}H_y(q)\longrightarrow0.
\]
A nonzero completely monotone function with finite value at the origin has a positive exponential lower envelope by the Hausdorff--Bernstein--Widder representation. Therefore
\[
\boxed{H_y\text{ is not completely monotone on }[0,\infty).}
\]
Thus the full complete-monotonicity/Gaussian-mixture route is CLOSED by a theorem-level no-go, while the proved first- and second-order inequalities remain valid. For each fixed admissible $y$, at least one signed derivative inequality fails at some order $m\ge3$.

The direct surviving G024 frontier is therefore one-dimensional Fourier/Wronskian positivity:
\[
\widehat D_y(x)>0
\quad\text{for all }x\in\mathbb R,\ 0<|y|<\frac12,
\]
equivalently
\[
\Re\!\left(f'(x+iy)^2-f(x+iy)f''(x+iy)\right)>0.
\]
The criterion above, quotient real-rootedness, full admissible Weil positivity, PF$_3$ and PF$_\infty$, and the reciprocal--conjugation zero-orbit implication remain OPEN. RH also remains OPEN. The monograph does not claim a proof of the Riemann Hypothesis.
