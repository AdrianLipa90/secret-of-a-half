\section{Publication v0.11 reciprocal--conjugation equivalence}

\[
u=\Omega(s),\qquad X(u)=\xi\!\left(\frac{u}{1+u}\right),
\]
and define the exact zero-set involutions
\[
\mathcal I(u)=1/u,\qquad \mathcal C(u)=\bar u.
\]
Then
\[
\mathcal I(u)=\mathcal C(u)
\iff |u|=1
\iff\Re s=\frac12.
\]
Hence
\[
\mathrm{RH}
\iff
\bigl[\forall u\in Z_X:\ \mathcal I(u)=\mathcal C(u)\bigr].
\]

\[
\Delta_{\mathrm{RC}}(u)=|1/u-\bar u|^2
\]

exact RH-equivalent reformulation.  At that stage the implication
\[
X(u)=0\Longrightarrow\Delta_{\mathrm{RC}}(u)=0
\]
had not yet been supplied by that route.  The current proof closes the
terminal channel through A0 in Chapters~58--59.
