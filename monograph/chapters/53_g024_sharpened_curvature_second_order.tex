\section{A stronger channelwise curvature floor}
For the theta channels of SOH-G004,
\[
-g_n''=h(r_n),
\qquad
h(r)=4r+\frac{24r}{(2r-3)^2},
\qquad
r_n=\pi n^2e^{2t}>3.
\]
For every $r>3$,
\[
\boxed{h(r)>19.}
\]
Indeed,
\[
h(r)-19=\frac{16r^3-124r^2+288r-171}{(2r-3)^2}.
\]
With $x=r-3\ge0$, the numerator is
\[
16x^3+20x^2-24x+9.
\]
The quadratic $20x^2-24x+9$ has negative discriminant and positive leading coefficient; adding $16x^3\ge0$ preserves positivity.

The G004 mixture identity is
\[
L''=\sum_np_n(-g_n'')-\operatorname{Var}_p(g_n'),
\]
and G004 proves $\operatorname{Var}_p(g_n')<2$. Therefore
\[
\boxed{L''(t)>17\qquad(t\in\mathbb R).}
\]

\section{Closing the band $1/3\le u\le1/2$}
The exact second-order normal form implies, after discarding only non-negative terms,
\[
\frac{4u^3H_y''}{H_y}>N_y+u\left[N_y^2-\mathbb E[B_u]\right].
\]
Using $N_y>33u$ and the preceding curvature bound,
\[
\frac{4u^3H_y''}{H_y}>u\left[33+1089u^2-79e^{2u}\right].
\]
Define
\[
F(u)=33+1089u^2-79e^{2u}.
\]
The exact rational endpoint estimates in the proof show that $F$ is positive on $1/3\le u\le1/2$. Therefore
\[
\boxed{H_y''(q)>0\qquad(1/9\le q\le1/4).}
\]
