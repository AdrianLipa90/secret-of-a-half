\chapter*{Preface and Reader's Guide}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Preface and Reader's Guide}

The purpose of this text is not to make the number $1/2$ look mysterious. The purpose is to separate three logically different questions that are often blended together:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Why is $1/2$ the centre of the zeta functional symmetry?
  \item Why does $1/2$ recur in binary balance, equal-amplitude cancellation, and spinorial sign closure?
  \item Why should every non-trivial zeta zero be fixed by the symmetry rather than merely paired by it?
\end{enumerate}
The first two questions admit exact answers. The third is the Riemann Hypothesis and remains open \citep{clay2026,bombieri2006}.

The text therefore uses a claim discipline. Every principal assertion is labelled as one of the following:
\begin{description}
  \item[Exact lemma or theorem.] A complete derivation is supplied from stated definitions.
  \item[Conditional theorem.] The conclusion is rigorous once its hypotheses are granted, but at least one hypothesis is not established.
  \item[Candidate route.] A construction is proposed with explicit success and failure conditions.
  \item[Interpretation.] A conceptual reading that is not itself a mathematical implication.
  \item[Numerical regression.] A finite computation checking code or an identity at sampled points.
\end{description}

The monograph is autonomous. It does not use another theoretical repository as an unstated dependency, and it does not import fitted constants. The independent objects are the classical zeta and xi functions, binary entropy, a two-dimensional Hilbert space, and explicit symmetry maps. This independence is deliberate: if the ansatz is valuable, it must survive on definitions that a reader can inspect without access to any external private formalism.

Readers interested only in the mathematical core may proceed directly to \cref{chap:symmetry,chap:entropy,chap:cancellation,chap:eta,chap:conditional}. Readers assessing publication claims should also read \cref{chap:nogo,chap:candidates,chap:falsification}. The appendices collect the elementary calculations, numerical tables, and a claim ledger.
