# SOH-G017 — V4 paired-spectrum orbit classification

Status: **THEOREM / exact set-theoretic reduction**

Version: 0.1

## Scope

This note classifies the finite paired spectrum introduced by SOH-G014 under the exact projective Klein-four action already established in SOH-G012.

No Riemann-Hypothesis claim is made. The status of the possible exceptional value

\[
F(-1/4)=\xi(1/2+i/2)
\]

is left **UNKNOWN**.

## Operators

Work in the affine \(s\)-chart with

\[
R(s)=1-s,
\qquad
N(s)=\frac{s-1}{2s-1},
\qquad
E(s)=\frac{s}{2s-1}.
\]

These are the \(s\)-plane representatives of the G012 projective operators

\[
R_u(u)=\frac1u,
\qquad
N_u(u)=-\frac1u,
\qquad
E_u(u)=-u.
\]

Direct calculation gives

\[
R^2=N^2=E^2=I,
\qquad
RN=NR=E,
\]

so

\[
V_4=\{I,R,N,E\}
\]

acts on the affine chart away from the shared pole \(s=1/2\) of \(N\) and \(E\).

## Paired xi spectrum

Define

\[
P_N:=\{\rho:\xi(\rho)=0,\ \xi(N(\rho))=0\}.
\]

SOH-G014 proved that \(P_N\) is finite.

### Proposition 1 — \(P_N\) is \(V_4\)-invariant

If \(s\in P_N\), then

\[
\xi(s)=0,
\qquad
\xi(N(s))=0.
\]

The completed xi functional equation gives

\[
\xi(R(s))=\xi(1-s)=\xi(s)=0.
\]

Because \(R\) and \(N\) commute,

\[
N(R(s))=R(N(s)),
\]

and therefore

\[
\xi(N(R(s)))=\xi(R(N(s)))=\xi(N(s))=0.
\]

Hence \(R(s)\in P_N\). Also \(N(s)\in P_N\) because \(N^2=I\), and \(E(s)=R(N(s))\in P_N\). Thus \(P_N\) is invariant under all of \(V_4\).

## Fixed loci of the three nontrivial involutions

### Reflection

\[
R(s)=s
\iff
s=\frac12.
\]

But SOH-G003 gives

\[
\xi\!\left(\frac12\right)=\int_0^\infty\Phi(y)\,dy>0.
\]

Therefore no point of \(P_N\) is fixed by \(R\).

### Euler half-turn

\[
E(s)=s
\iff
s\in\{0,1\}.
\]

The completed xi function satisfies

\[
\xi(0)=\xi(1)=\frac12,
\]

so no point of \(P_N\) is fixed by \(E\).

### Negative inversion

\[
N(s)=s
\iff
2s^2-2s+1=0,
\]

hence

\[
s_\pm=\frac12\pm\frac{i}{2}.
\]

These two points are exchanged by \(R\):

\[
R(s_+)=s_-,
\qquad
R(s_-)=s_+.
\]

Their zero status is not established here.

## Theorem — complete orbit classification

Every \(V_4\)-orbit contained in \(P_N\) has cardinality \(4\), except possibly the orbit

\[
\mathcal O_*=\left\{\frac12+\frac i2,\frac12-\frac i2\right\},
\]

which has cardinality \(2\) if and only if either, equivalently both, of its points are xi zeros.

### Proof

For a finite group action, orbit size is

\[
|V_4\cdot s|=\frac{|V_4|}{|\operatorname{Stab}(s)|}.
\]

The only possible nontrivial stabilizers are generated by \(R\), \(N\), or \(E\).

The fixed loci of \(R\) and \(E\) contain no xi zeros, as shown above. Therefore a point of \(P_N\) can have nontrivial stabilizer only if it is fixed by \(N\), which forces

\[
s\in\left\{\frac12+\frac i2,\frac12-\frac i2\right\}.
\]

At either such point the stabilizer has order \(2\), generated by \(N\), and the orbit has size \(4/2=2\). All remaining stabilizers are trivial, giving orbit size \(4\). QED.

## Corollary — exact cardinality congruence

There exist an integer \(a\ge0\) and a bit

\[
\varepsilon\in\{0,1\}
\]

such that

\[
\boxed{|P_N|=4a+2\varepsilon}.
\]

Here

\[
\varepsilon=1
\]

if and only if

\[
\xi\!\left(\frac12+\frac i2\right)=0.
\]

Equivalently,

\[
|P_N|\equiv
\begin{cases}
0\pmod4,&\varepsilon=0,\\
2\pmod4,&\varepsilon=1.
\end{cases}
\]

## Quotient cross-check with G016

Under

\[
q(s)=\left(s-\frac12\right)^2,
\]

SOH-G016 proved

\[
q(P_N)=P_J
\]

with exact \(2{:}1\) fibers. Therefore

\[
|P_J|=\frac{|P_N|}{2}=2a+\varepsilon.
\]

Hence

\[
\boxed{|P_J|\equiv\varepsilon\pmod2}.
\]

The exceptional \(N\)-fixed pair maps to the unique still-allowed quotient fixed value:

\[
q\!\left(\frac12\pm\frac i2\right)=-\frac14.
\]

Thus the same bit is equivalently

\[
\varepsilon=1
\iff
F(-1/4)=0.
\]

The equality is a correspondence statement only; this note does not decide the value of \(F(-1/4)\).

## Claim firewall

PROVED here:

- \(P_N\) is \(V_4\)-invariant;
- only \(N\)-fixed points can support a non-generic paired-spectrum orbit;
- every generic orbit has size \(4\);
- the only possible size-\(2\) orbit is \(\{1/2\pm i/2\}\);
- \(|P_N|=4a+2\varepsilon\);
- via G016, \(|P_J|=2a+\varepsilon\).

NOT proved here:

- that \(P_N\) or \(P_J\) is nonempty;
- that \(\xi(1/2+i/2)=0\) or is nonzero by a new analytic theorem;
- that \(F(-1/4)=0\) or is nonzero by a new analytic theorem;
- real-rootedness of \(F\);
- PF-infinity;
- SOH-G003 real-rootedness frontier;
- RH.
