\section{Pairs are typed relations}
The phrase ``every structure has a pair'' is useful only after the word
\emph{pair} is typed. The half-axis is related to complement by a fixed-point
relation, to Shannon entropy by an extremal relation, to the Bloch equator by a
representation map, and to destructive interference by a cancellation theorem.
These are distinct edges. The representation is therefore a graph
\[
 \mathcal G=(V,E),
\]
with $\deg(v)\ge1$ for every declared semantic node, not a partition into
exclusive two-element sets.

We distinguish fixed-point, dual, implication, representation, holonomic, and
cross-reference edges. A semantic pair does not automatically carry a phase:
holonomy is meaningful only when a connection and a closed or composable path
have been specified.

\section{Normalized holonomy}
At the primitive level let an eligible edge carry
\[
 h(e)\in\mathbb R/\mathbb Z
\]
in turns. A half-turn has $h=1/2$; a full closure has $h=0$ modulo one. For a
composable path $P$,
\begin{equation}
 h(P)=\sum_{e\in P}h(e)\pmod1,
 \qquad
 \mathcal U(P)=\exp(2\pi i\,h(P)).
 \label{eq:path-holonomy-sum}
\end{equation}
The complex representative uses radians; path composition does not require
radians as a primitive.

Berry phase is a standard $U(1)$ connection holonomy on a quantum-state bundle
\citep{berry1984,simon1983}. The Aharonov--Bohm phase is likewise a $U(1)$
holonomy of an electromagnetic connection \citep{aharonovbohm1959}. Their
common bundle structure does not identify their physical potentials.

\section{The phase crystal}
Let
\[
 \mathbb T^N=(\mathbb R/\mathbb Z)^N
\]
with $N=36$ in the current PhaseNav geometry. For $q,r\in\mathbb T^N$ define
\[
 \Delta_k(q,r)=
 \left(r_k-q_k+\frac12\right)\bmod1-\frac12
\]
and
\[
 \mathbf v(q\to r)=(\Delta_1,\ldots,\Delta_N)\in[-1/2,1/2)^N.
\]
This local tangent/log-map vectorization generates negative and positive
components as opposite shortest-path orientations and zero as coincident
coordinates. Exactly antipodal coordinates require a branch convention.

Once a tangent vector exists, local inner products, projections,
orthogonalization, curvature estimates, constraints, density, and viscosity can
be defined downstream. The architectural direction is metric/connection
$\to$ tangent relation $\to$ vector operator, not the reverse.

\section{Holonomy eligibility}
\begin{criterion}[Holonomy eligibility]
\label{crit:holonomy-eligibility}
A semantic edge may carry canonical holonomy only if:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(H\arabic*)]
 \item a connection or equivalent phase-transport law is specified;
 \item the edge belongs to a closed loop or composable path with endpoint convention;
 \item the resulting phase is gauge invariant under the permitted changes, or
 its gauge dependence is explicitly part of the object;
 \item its claim status is independent of a purely generated semantic embedding.
\end{enumerate}
\end{criterion}
Thus paired sectors such as energy--matter or time--space do not automatically
receive a $\pi$ phase.
