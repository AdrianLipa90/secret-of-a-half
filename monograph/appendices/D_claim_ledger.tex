\section{Promotion and proof firewall}

An OPEN claim may be promoted only when its complete proof or reproducible
construction is present, every dependency is explicit, and no dependency is
merely an RH-equivalent statement left unproved. Numerical agreement cannot
promote a claim to exact status. The current terminal obstruction remains
SOH-C005; therefore the Riemann Hypothesis remains OPEN in this monograph.
