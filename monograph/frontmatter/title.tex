\textbf{Research status.} This monograph develops an autonomous structural ansatz. It proves a collection of elementary, geometric and finite-section analytic lemmas, states conditional critical-axis theorems, and identifies the exact bridges that remain open. Version 0.7 adds an identity-axis formulation, normalized recurrence before radians, a typed semantic/holonomy graph, geometry-first CIEL/PhaseNav dependencies, and an executable solver that prevents model or open premises from being silently promoted. It does not claim a proof of the Riemann Hypothesis.
