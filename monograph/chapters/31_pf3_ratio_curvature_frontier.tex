\chapter{PF3 Ratio-Curvature Frontier}
\label{chap:pf3-ratio-curvature}

\status{Exact algebraic reduction proved; PF3 remains open.}

The previous chapter proves that the normalized Taylor coefficients
\[
F(w)=\sum_{k\ge0}a_k w^k,
\qquad
 a_k=\frac{m_k}{(2k)!},
\]
form a PF$_2$ sequence.  Equivalently, with
\[
r_k:=\frac{a_k}{a_{k-1}},
\]
the ratios $r_k$ are nonincreasing.

\section{The first genuinely new minor}

For $k\ge2$ define the solid order-three Toeplitz minor
\[
\Delta_k=
\det\begin{pmatrix}
 a_k&a_{k+1}&a_{k+2}\\
 a_{k-1}&a_k&a_{k+1}\\
 a_{k-2}&a_{k-1}&a_k
\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Direct expansion gives
\[
\Delta_k=
-a_{k-2}a_ka_{k+2}
+a_{k-2}a_{k+1}^2
+a_{k-1}^2a_{k+2}
-2a_{k-1}a_ka_{k+1}
+a_k^3.
\]

Set
\[
u_k=\frac{r_k}{r_{k-1}},\qquad
v_k=\frac{r_{k+1}}{r_k},\qquad
w_k=\frac{r_{k+2}}{r_{k+1}}.
\]
After division by a strictly positive factor, the sign of $\Delta_k$ is the sign of
\[
\boxed{
M_k=1-2v_k+v_k^2(u_k+w_k-u_kw_k).
}
\]
Hence
\[
\boxed{\Delta_k\ge0\iff M_k\ge0.}
\]

\section{Why PF2 does not already imply PF3}

PF$_2$ supplies only
\[
0<u_k,v_k,w_k\le1.
\]
Those inequalities do not force $M_k\ge0$.  Thus PF$_3$ requires a new quantitative property: control of the discrete curvature of the adjacent ratio sequence, not merely monotonicity of the ratios themselves.

\section{Finite diagnostic}

A high-precision falsification screen evaluates $u_k,v_k,w_k$, the exact margin $M_k$, and the corresponding Toeplitz determinant, and verifies numerical reconstruction of the determinant from the ratio formula.  In the sampled range the observed pattern is
\[
u_k<v_k<w_k<1,
\qquad
M_k>0.
\]
This is explicitly a finite diagnostic and is not promoted to a theorem.

\section{Open analytic target}

Let
\[
R(p)=\frac{1}{\Gamma(p+1)}\int_0^\infty y^p\Phi(y)\,dy,
\qquad a_k=R(2k).
\]
The SOH-G005 theorem gives concavity of $\log R$ and therefore PF$_2$.  The next target is a quantitative bound on the change of the adjacent ratios strong enough to imply
\[
M_k\ge0\quad\text{for every }k.
\]

\begin{warning}
The exact reduction of PF$_3$ to the ratio-curvature margin is proved.  Global positivity of that margin, all order-three Toeplitz minors, PF$_3$, PF$_\infty$, SOH-G003 real-rootedness, and the Riemann Hypothesis remain open.
\end{warning}
