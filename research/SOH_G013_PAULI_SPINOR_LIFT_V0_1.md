# SOH-G013 — Pauli / SU(2) Spinor Lift of the Projective Operator Algebra

## Status

**THEOREM-LEVEL CP1 / SU(2) DOUBLE-COVER ALGEBRA / PROVED.**

SOH-G012 established the projective operator group generated by

\[
R(u)=\frac1u,
\qquad
E(u)=-u,
\qquad
N(u)=-\frac1u,
\]

with

\[
R^2=E^2=N^2=I,
\qquad
RE=ER=N,
\]

so that

\[
\{I,R,E,N\}\cong V_4.
\]

SOH-G013 lifts these CP1 transformations to determinant-one unitary Pauli matrices. The lift is exact and does not introduce a new xi symmetry or any RH claim.

## 1. Pauli representatives

Use

\[
\sigma_x=
\begin{pmatrix}
0&1\\
1&0
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
\sigma_y=
\begin{pmatrix}
0&-i\\
i&0
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
\sigma_z=
\begin{pmatrix}
1&0\\
0&-1
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

A matrix

\[
M=\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{pmatrix}
\]

acts projectively by

\[
u\longmapsto\frac{au+b}{cu+d}.
\]

The G012 maps have Pauli projective representatives

\[
R\sim\sigma_x,
\qquad
E\sim\sigma_z,
\qquad
N\sim-i\sigma_y.
\]

Multiplication by a nonzero scalar does not change the induced CP1 map.

## 2. Determinant-one unitary lifts

Choose

\[
\boxed{\widetilde R=i\sigma_x},
\qquad
\boxed{\widetilde E=i\sigma_z},
\qquad
\boxed{\widetilde N=i\sigma_y}.
\]

Each matrix lies in `SU(2)`:

\[
\widetilde A^\dagger\widetilde A=I,
\qquad
\det\widetilde A=1,
\qquad
A\in\{R,E,N\}.
\]

Their projective actions are exactly

\[
\widetilde R:u\mapsto1/u,
\qquad
\widetilde E:u\mapsto-u,
\qquad
\widetilde N:u\mapsto-1/u.
\]

## 3. Quaternion multiplication

The Pauli multiplication law gives

\[
\widetilde R\widetilde E=\widetilde N,
\qquad
\widetilde E\widetilde R=-\widetilde N,
\]

and cyclically

\[
\widetilde E\widetilde N=\widetilde R,
\qquad
\widetilde N\widetilde E=-\widetilde R,
\]

\[
\widetilde N\widetilde R=\widetilde E,
\qquad
\widetilde R\widetilde N=-\widetilde E.
\]

Moreover,

\[
\boxed{
\widetilde R^2
=\widetilde E^2
=\widetilde N^2
=-I.
}
\]

Therefore every non-central generator has order four in the spinor lift and the generated subgroup is

\[
\boxed{
Q_8=\{\pm I,\pm i\sigma_x,\pm i\sigma_y,\pm i\sigma_z\}.
}
\]

## 4. Projectivization recovers G012 exactly

The center of `Q8` is

\[
Z(Q_8)=\{\pm I\}.
\]

The matrices `M` and `-M` induce the same Möbius transformation. Hence the projective map has kernel exactly `±I`, and

\[
\boxed{
Q_8/\{\pm I\}\cong V_4.
}
\]

Equivalently there is an exact short sequence

\[
\boxed{
1\longrightarrow\{\pm I\}
\longrightarrow Q_8
\longrightarrow V_4
\longrightarrow1.
}
\]

The apparent commutativity of the G012 maps is therefore the projective image of anticommuting spinor lifts. For example,

\[
\widetilde R\widetilde E
=-\widetilde E\widetilde R,
\]

but the central sign `-I` disappears on CP1.

## 5. Spinorial order doubling

For each Pauli axis,

\[
U_j(\pi)
=\exp\!\left(-\frac{i\pi}{2}\sigma_j\right)
=-i\sigma_j.
\]

This differs from the chosen `+i sigma_j` lift only by the central matrix `-I`, so it induces the same projective transformation.

The exact order relations are

\[
U_j(\pi)^2=-I,
\qquad
U_j(\pi)^4=I.
\]

Thus the corresponding CP1 operation has order two while its spinor lift has order four. A projective `2π` return carries the central spinor sign `-I`; identity in the lifted spinor requires the full `4π` cycle.

## 6. Fixed pairs and the Bloch axes

Using the homogeneous spinor chart `(u,1)^T`, the Bloch vector is

\[
\mathbf b(u)
=\frac{1}{1+|u|^2}
\left(
2\Re u,
-2\Im u,
|u|^2-1
\right).
\]

The three projective Pauli operators have the fixed pairs

\[
R:\quad u=\pm1,
\]

\[
E:\quad u=0,\infty,
\]

\[
N:\quad u=\pm i.
\]

Their Bloch images are exactly the three antipodal coordinate-axis pairs:

- `R`: `±x`,
- `E`: `±z`,
- `N`: `±y` (with orientation determined by the chosen chart convention).

The G012 negative-inversion fixed pair `u=±i`, which maps to

\[
s=\frac12\pm\frac{i}{2},
\]

is therefore precisely the Pauli-`y` eigenline pair in this CP1 chart.

## 7. Proof firewall

Proved in SOH-G013:

- the G012 operators admit the stated Pauli representatives;
- `i sigma_x`, `i sigma_z`, and `i sigma_y` are `SU(2)` lifts of `R`, `E`, and `N`;
- the lifts generate `Q8`;
- each non-central lift squares to `-I` and has order four;
- projectivization identifies `±M` and gives `Q8/{±I} ~= V4`;
- the apparent projective commutativity comes from a central spinor sign;
- the standard `pi` Pauli rotations differ from the chosen lifts only by `-I`;
- the fixed pairs are the three antipodal Bloch-axis pairs.

Not proved in SOH-G013:

- any new functional equation for xi;
- invariance of the xi zero set under `E` or `N`;
- that a non-trivial xi zero must be a Pauli fixed line;
- PF-infinity;
- SOH-G003 real-rootedness;
- the Riemann hypothesis.
