The purpose of this text is to keep distinct three questions that are easily conflated:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Why is $1/2$ the centre of the functional symmetry of the completed zeta function?
  \item Why does the same half recur in binary balance, equal-amplitude cancellation, projective self-duality, and spinorial phase closure?
  \item What additional analytic mechanism, if any, forces every non-trivial zeta zero onto that axis?
\end{enumerate}
The first two questions admit exact answers in the declared mathematical representations. The third contains the Riemann-Hypothesis difficulty and remains open \citep{clay2026,bombieri2006}.

Version 0.9 Integrated Canon V3 applies a typed claim discipline throughout the monograph:
\begin{description}
  \item[Exact or standard.] A complete derivation is supplied from stated definitions, or a classical theorem is invoked with its assumptions and citation.
  \item[Conditional.] The conclusion follows rigorously once a named unproved hypothesis is granted.
  \item[Model.] A TIR/PhaseNav or information-spinor assignment used only inside an explicitly declared modelling layer.
  \item[Open.] A missing bridge that is never promoted automatically.
  \item[Numerical.] A finite computation, regression check, or certificate with a stated finite scope.
\end{description}
Whenever the prose adds an interpretive reading rather than a mathematical implication, it is explicitly marked \textbf{INTERPRETACJA}. This label is not a proof status; it is a semantic firewall separating interpretation from theorem content.

The canonical claim map is cumulative through SOH-G023. SOH-L001--SOH-L032 retain the stable foundational and Li/Weil meanings recorded in \cref{app:claims}. SOH-G024 is present on this branch as a separately guarded candidate and is not promoted into the canonical claim ledger. Historical identifiers that collided during development remain provenance aliases only; they do not redefine active claims.

The G024 branch deliberately distinguishes two different $y$-tilts. The external Dimitrov--Xu family is $D_y(u)=\cosh(2yu)C(u)$ and is tied directly to the Wiener density criterion. The internal family $J_y$ carries the complex Jensen/Laguerre functional. They coincide at $y=0$ but are not identified for $y\ne0$. Complete monotonicity of $H_y(q)=D_y(\sqrt q)$ would be sufficient for RH through a positive Gaussian-mixture representation, but that complete-monotonicity statement is an OPEN premise. Finite derivative checks do not change its status.

The proof firewall is therefore multi-branch but consistent. SOH-C001 remains open for the specific normalized information-spinor state-map programme. SOH-C005 remains the open global Weil/Li positivity frontier. In the quotient line, SOH-G003 real-rootedness remains open; PF$_3$ for the actual quotient coefficients and PF$_\infty$ remain open. SOH-G021 closes only the abstract implication from the G020 structural package to PF$_3$. SOH-G022 proves a local sufficient condition for the G006 solid margin. SOH-G023 rewrites that condition without proving the required global increment law. SOH-G024 supplies a direct exact reduction plus an unproved sufficient premise. The negative-inversion root-invariance route, by contrast, is closed negatively by SOH-G018. None of these distinctions changes the final status: the Riemann Hypothesis remains OPEN in this monograph.
