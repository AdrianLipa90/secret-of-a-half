\[

\[
\boxed{\mathcal I(u)=\mathcal C(u)\iff |u|=1\iff\Re s=\frac12.}

\[
\boxed{\mathrm{RH}\iff \forall u\in Z_X:\;u^{-1}=\bar u.}
