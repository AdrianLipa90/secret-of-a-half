\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}

This monograph studies why the value $1/2$ is structurally distinguished in the analytic geometry surrounding the Riemann zeta function while maintaining a strict separation between exact mathematics, finite numerical diagnostics, software/model constructions, conditional implications, computer-assisted certificates, and open proof obligations.

The foundational layer proves several exact selections of the half. Binary Shannon entropy is uniquely maximal at $\sigma=1/2$ with value $\ln2$; normalized equal-gain two-channel cancellation occurs exactly at equal weights and relative phase $\pi$; and the conjugate-affine involution $s\mapsto1-\bar s$ has fixed line $\Re s=1/2$. These statements explain structural coincidence inside their declared representations but do not by themselves locate every non-trivial zeta zero.

The analytic programme then develops three principal lines. First, the Li/Weil and PhaseNav line places the projective coordinate $\Omega(s)=s/(1-s)$, Li's coordinate $1-1/s=-1/\Omega(s)$, localized Weil operators, a Hermite dense core, and certified prime-tail controls in one audited framework. Full admissible Weil positivity SOH-C005 remains an RH-equivalent open frontier.

Second, the centred quotient
\[
\xi\!\left(\frac12+z\right)=F(z^2)
\]
converts the Riemann Hypothesis into real-rootedness of the entire function $F$. The monograph derives the exact positive Riemann kernel, proves the declared log-concavity and PF$_2$ coefficient results, and develops PF$_3$ obstructions. SOH-G021 proves that positivity, half-mass normalization, strict coefficient decay, PF$_2$, and the normalized envelope do not jointly imply PF$_3$. SOH-G022 gives an exact local decomposition of the solid PF$_3$ margin, while SOH-G023 rewrites that margin using the reciprocal curvature deficit $E_k=(1-q_k)^{-1}$. Actual PF$_3$, PF$_\infty$, and SOH-G003 real-rootedness remain open.

Third, the negative-inversion line establishes exact projective and spinorial algebra but closes the proposed zero-pairing shortcut negatively: SOH-G018 proves that the relevant quotient disk is zero-free and that canonical negative inversion maps no zero of $F$ or $\xi$ to another zero. The geometry is retained; the spectral shortcut is not.

SOH-G024, now integrated into the repository mainline while remaining separately non-canonical in the mathematical promotion ledger, returns directly to the Jensen--Wiener correlation-kernel frontier. For the even full-line Riemann kernel
\[
K(t)=\frac12\Phi(|t|),
\]
define
\[
C(u)=\int_{\mathbb R}r^2K(u+r)K(u-r)\,dr,
\qquad
D_y(u)=\cosh(2yu)C(u).
\]
The Dimitrov--Xu correlation criterion together with Wiener's $L^1$ Tauberian theorem reduces RH to strict Fourier positivity of $D_y$ for every real frequency and every $0<|y|<1/2$ \citep{dimitrovxu2016,wiener1932tauberian}. The external Wronskian identity is
\[
4\widehat D_y(2x)=\Re\!\left(f'(x+iy)^2-f(x+iy)f''(x+iy)\right).
\]
A sufficient route is complete monotonicity of
\[
H_y(q)=D_y(\sqrt q).
\]
The G024 sharpening of the channel-curvature argument proves
\[
\boxed{-(\log K)''>17},
\qquad
\boxed{-\frac{H_y'}{H_y}>\frac{33}{2}},
\]
so $H_y'(q)<0$ globally throughout the full Dimitrov--Xu strip. The normalized bridge measure further yields
\[
\mathbb E[r^{2n}]<\frac{(2n+1)!!}{34^n},
\qquad
\mathbb E[e^{\lambda r^2}]\le(1-\lambda/17)^{-3/2}.
\]

The second complete-monotonicity inequality is also closed globally. Analytic estimates first prove $H_y''(q)>0$ outside a compact core. SOH-G024-Q then certifies
\[
\boxed{L''''(t)<20L''(t)},\qquad L=-\log K,
\]
using outward \texttt{mpmath.iv} interval arithmetic on $0\le t\le2/5$, explicit low theta channels, an analytic enclosure of the infinite tail, and a separate analytic proof for $t\ge2/5$. The downstream bridge/Riccati argument is exact and yields the strict compact gap
\[
S_y^2-S_y'>\frac{793}{48}>0.
\]
Hence
\[
\boxed{H_y''(q)>0\qquad(q\ge0,\ 0<|y|<1/2).}
\]
The computer-assisted trust boundary is explicit and follows validated-numerics methodology rather than finite-grid inference \citep{moore2009interval}.

The next direct frontier is third order. SOH-G024-S proves the exact recurrence
\[
F_{m+1}=S_yF_m-F_m',
\qquad F_m=(-1)^m\frac{H_y^{(m)}}{H_y},
\]
and reduces the third-order derivative to the bridge cumulant--covariance identity
\[
R''=\mathbb E[C_u^{(3)}]-3\operatorname{Cov}(A_u,B_u)+\mu_3(A_u),
\]
or equivalently to a logarithmic-growth barrier for the already positive second-order margin. The global third-order sign remains open.

The present proof boundary is therefore explicit. The monograph proves the stated geometric, quotient, kernel, PF$_2$, zero-exclusion, coefficient-normalization, G021 no-go, G022--G023 normal-form, and G024 first/second complete-monotonicity results at their declared evidential levels. It does not prove complete monotonicity in orders $m\ge3$, strict external Fourier positivity on the full strip, actual PF$_3$, PF$_\infty$, SOH-G003, SOH-C005, or the Riemann Hypothesis. RH remains OPEN.
