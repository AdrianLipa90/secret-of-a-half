\section{Current promotion and proof firewall}

An OPEN claim may be promoted only when its complete proof or reproducible construction is present, every dependency is explicit, and no dependency is merely an RH-equivalent statement left unproved. Numerical agreement cannot promote a claim to exact status. Interpretive prose cannot promote a claim either and is marked \textbf{INTERPRETACJA} when present.

The current integrated frontier is not one single missing lemma. SOH-C001 remains open for the normalized state-map branch; SOH-C005 remains the RH-equivalent global Weil/Li positivity frontier; SOH-G003 remains the RH-equivalent quotient real-rootedness frontier; PF$_3$ and PF$_\infty$ for the actual quotient coefficients remain open. SOH-G021 closes only the shortcut from the abstract G020 structural package to PF$_3$. SOH-G022 proves a local sufficient condition for the solid G006 PF$_3$ margin, but the required ratio-curvature inequalities are not proved globally for the actual coefficients and a separate theorem is still required to upgrade solid-minor control to full PF$_3$. By contrast, negative-inversion root invariance is not open: SOH-G018 proves that the paired sets are empty. The Riemann Hypothesis remains OPEN in this monograph.
