\[

\text{zero has no independent realization; a zero of an admitted observable

coordinate convention, an energy ansatz, or a fitted physical model.

\[
\boxed{
\xi(\rho)=0
\stackrel{\mathrm{A0}}{\Longrightarrow}
E_{\rm rel}(\rho)=0
\stackrel{\rm coercivity}{\Longrightarrow}
\Delta_{\rm RC}(\Omega(\rho))=0
\Longrightarrow
\Re\rho=\frac12.
}
\]

The two nontrivial arrows after A0 are exact:
\[
E_{\rm rel}(s)
\ge
D_H(\Re s)
\ge
\frac{|s|^2|1-s|^2}{2}\,
\Delta_{\rm RC}(\Omega(s)),
\]
and
\[
\Delta_{\rm RC}(\Omega(s))=0
\iff
\Re s=\frac12.
\]
