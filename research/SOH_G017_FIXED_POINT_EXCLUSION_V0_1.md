# SOH-G017 — Fixed-Point Exclusion and Exceptional-Orbit Divisibility

**Claim status:** PROVED from the previously established positive Riemann kernel and elementary inequalities. No table of zeta zeros is used.

## 1. Target

G015 introduced the quotient involution

\[
J(w)=\frac{1}{16w}
\]

for the even entire function

\[
\xi\!\left(\frac12+z\right)=F(z^2).
\]

Its fixed values are

\[
w=\pm\frac14.
\]

The positive fixed value was already excluded because the G003 coefficient theorem gives

\[
F(x)>0\qquad(x\ge 0).
\]

The only unresolved fixed-point candidate was therefore

\[
w=-\frac14.
\]

Since \(z^2=-1/4\) gives \(z=\pm i/2\),

\[
F\!\left(-\frac14\right)
=\xi\!\left(\frac12+\frac{i}{2}\right)
=:\Xi\!\left(\frac12\right).
\]

## 2. Positive-kernel representation

From SOH-G003,

\[
\Xi(t)=\int_0^\infty \Phi(y)\cos(ty)\,dy,
\]

with

\[
\Phi(y)=\sum_{n\ge1}\phi_n(y),
\]

\[
\phi_n(y)=4\pi n^2 e^{5y/2}
\left(2\pi n^2e^{2y}-3\right)
 e^{-\pi n^2e^{2y}}>0
\qquad(y\ge0).
\]

At \(t=1/2\),

\[
F\!\left(-\frac14\right)
=\int_0^\infty\Phi(y)\cos(y/2)\,dy.
\]

Split the integral into

\[
[0,1/2]\cup[1/2,\pi]\cup[\pi,\infty).
\]

The middle interval is nonnegative because \(\Phi>0\) and \(\cos(y/2)\ge0\) for \(0\le y\le\pi\).

## 3. Explicit positive block

On \(0\le y\le1/2\), retain only the \(n=1\) channel. There,

\[
e^{5y/2}\ge1,
\qquad
2\pi e^{2y}-3\ge2\pi-3,
\qquad
 e^{-\pi e^{2y}}\ge e^{-\pi e},
\]

and

\[
\cos(y/2)\ge\cos(1/4).
\]

Hence

\[
\int_0^{1/2}\Phi(y)\cos(y/2)\,dy
\ge A,
\]

where

\[
A:=2\pi(2\pi-3)e^{-\pi e}\cos(1/4).
\]

Using only

\[
3<\pi<4,
\qquad
2<e<3,
\qquad
\cos x\ge1-\frac{x^2}{2},
\]

we obtain

\[
A>
18\cdot\frac{31}{32}\cdot3^{-12}.
\]

The exact integer comparison

\[
18\cdot31\cdot2^{15}
>
32\cdot3^{12}
\]

therefore yields

\[
\boxed{A>2^{-15}}.
\]

## 4. Super-exponential tail bound

For \(y\ge\pi\), write

\[
r_n=\pi n^2e^{2y}.
\]

Since \(2r_n-3<2r_n\),

\[
\phi_n(y)
<8\pi^2n^4e^{9y/2}e^{-\pi n^2e^{2y}}.
\]

Set

\[
x=e^{2y},
\qquad
X=e^{2\pi}.
\]

Then

\[
\int_\pi^\infty\phi_n(y)\,dy
<4\pi^2n^4
\int_X^\infty x^{5/4}e^{-\pi n^2x}\,dx.
\]

For \(x\ge X\),

\[
\frac{d}{dx}\log\left(x^{5/4}e^{-\pi n^2x}\right)
=\frac{5}{4x}-\pi n^2
\le-\frac{\pi n^2}{2},
\]

so

\[
\int_X^\infty x^{5/4}e^{-\pi n^2x}\,dx
\le
\frac{2}{\pi n^2}X^{5/4}e^{-\pi n^2X}.
\]

Therefore

\[
\int_\pi^\infty\Phi(y)\,dy
\le T,
\]

with

\[
T:=8\pi X^{5/4}
\sum_{n\ge1}n^2e^{-\pi Xn^2}.
\]

Let

\[
q=e^{-\pi X}.
\]

Because \(n^2\ge n\),

\[
\sum_{n\ge1}n^2e^{-\pi Xn^2}
\le
\sum_{n\ge1}n^2q^n
=
\frac{q(1+q)}{(1-q)^3}.
\]

Thus

\[
T\le
8\pi X^{5/4}
\frac{q(1+q)}{(1-q)^3}.
\]

For a completely elementary coarse bound, use

\[
X=e^{2\pi}>e^6>2^6=64,
\]

so

\[
q=e^{-\pi X}<e^{-192}<2^{-192}.
\]

Also

\[
X=e^{2\pi}<e^8<3^8,
\]

and \(q<1/2\), hence

\[
\frac{1+q}{(1-q)^3}<12.
\]

Consequently

\[
T<32\cdot3^{10}\cdot12\cdot2^{-192}.
\]

The exact integer inequality

\[
32\cdot3^{10}\cdot12<2^{25}
\]

gives

\[
\boxed{T<2^{-167}}.
\]

## 5. Fixed-point exclusion theorem

The middle block \([1/2,\pi]\) is nonnegative, while the absolute magnitude of every possibly negative contribution beyond \(\pi\) is bounded by \(T\). Therefore

\[
F\!\left(-\frac14\right)
\ge A-T
>
2^{-15}-2^{-167}
>0.
\]

Hence

\[
\boxed{F(-1/4)>0}.
\]

Together with the already proved \(F(1/4)>0\), neither fixed point of \(J\) is a root of \(F\).

## 6. Exceptional-orbit corollaries

Let

\[
P_J=\{w:F(w)=0,\;F(J(w))=0\}.
\]

G015 proves that \(P_J\) is finite. Since \(J^2=\mathrm{id}\), \(J\) acts on \(P_J\). G017 proves that this action has no fixed points. Therefore \(P_J\) is a disjoint union of two-cycles and

\[
\boxed{|P_J|\equiv0\pmod2}.
\]

G016 proves that the quotient map

\[
q(s)=\left(s-\frac12\right)^2
\]

maps the paired xi set \(P_N\) exactly two-to-one onto \(P_J\):

\[
|P_N|=2|P_J|.
\]

Hence

\[
\boxed{|P_N|\equiv0\pmod4}.
\]

More precisely, every \(J\)-two-cycle lifts to four distinct points in the \(s\)-plane, forming one full orbit of the projective Klein-four action generated by reflection \(s\mapsto1-s\) and the canonical negative inversion.

## 7. Proof firewall

G017 proves only:

- \(F(-1/4)>0\);
- neither fixed point \(\pm1/4\) of \(J\) belongs to the root set of \(F\);
- the finite exceptional set \(P_J\), if nonempty, is a union of two-cycles;
- \(|P_J|\) is even;
- \(|P_N|\) is divisible by four;
- each exceptional quotient two-cycle lifts to a four-point projective orbit.

G017 does **not** prove:

- \(P_J=\varnothing\) or \(P_N=\varnothing\);
- invariance of the complete zero set under negative inversion;
- real-rootedness of \(F\);
- PF-infinity;
- SOH-G003;
- the Riemann Hypothesis.
