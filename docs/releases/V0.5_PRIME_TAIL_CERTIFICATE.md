Version 0.5 adds an analytic cutoff-removal certificate to the PhaseNav–Weil Hermite programme.

The infinite prime-power tail is transformed through `u=log(x)` and the reciprocal compactification `z_tail=1/u`. The resulting positive majorant has an exact upper-incomplete-gamma form. For the declared `N<=6`, `Q=100000`, `w=0.8` profile, the omitted-tail matrix has certified spectral-norm envelope at most `7.717202888999335e-13`.

This closes controlled cutoff removal for each fixed declared finite section. Uniform basis-size control, global positivity and the null-structure implication to native closure remain open. No proof of the Riemann Hypothesis is claimed.
