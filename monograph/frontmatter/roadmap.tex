\section*{Current dependency logic}
The principal terminal criteria form an RH-equivalent cluster rather than a proof cycle:
\[
\text{orbit collapse}
\Longleftrightarrow
\text{SOH-G003 real-rootedness}
\Longleftrightarrow
\text{direct G024 Fourier positivity}
\Longleftrightarrow
\text{RH},
\]
with the usual hypotheses of each criterion. Cycling inside this cluster does not prove any member. A valid proof needs a non-circular incoming theorem.

The earlier all-order complete-monotonicity route is no longer an open bridge:
\[
\boxed{\text{full complete monotonicity of }H_y\;\text{is impossible by SOH-G024-T}.}
\]
Chapters~55 and 56 should therefore be read together: Chapter~55 retains exact derivative identities; Chapter~56 proves that the all-order sign hierarchy must eventually fail.

\section*{Recommended reading order}
For the shortest current proof frontier, read Chapters~1--3, 25--31, 37--46, and 50--57, followed by Appendices~D--E and the final synthesis. Chapter~57 is the cleanest projective formulation of the remaining zero-orbit burden; Chapter~56 is the publication-critical closure of the obsolete complete-monotonicity route.
