- Added an exponential basis-adaptive cutoff schedule for the PhaseNav–Weil Hermite tail.
- Proved a coarse envelope with asymptotic form
  `-c^2 N^2/(4w^2) + O(N log N)` in the logarithm.
- Added the exact statement that every schedule `Q_N=exp(cN)`, `c>0`, forces this envelope to zero.
- Audited the sharp v0.5 certificate for `N=1..20` using `log Q_N=max(log 100000,2N)`.
- Recorded maximum certified norm `3.280365246530569e-14` at `N=5`.
- Added native PhaseNav source, executor, receipt, tests, documentation and monograph Chapter 20.
- Added `SOH-L021` and `SOH-N006`.

Fixed-cutoff uniformity, global positivity, form closure and the null-space bridge remain open. Version 0.6 does not claim a proof of the Riemann Hypothesis.
