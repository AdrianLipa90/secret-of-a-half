\chapter{The PhaseNav--Weil Hermite Ladder}
\label{ch:hermite-ladder}

The two-channel arithmetic witness validates one localized direction of the
Weil form.  This chapter replaces that isolated direction by an explicit dense
ladder.  For fixed $w>0$ and real $\gamma$, define
\[
 \psi_n(r)=
 \left(\frac{w}{\sqrt\pi\,2^n n!}\right)^{1/2}
 H_n(w(r-\gamma))e^{-w^2(r-\gamma)^2/2}.
\]
The translated-scaled Hermite family is a Schwartz basis.  Consequently the
union of its finite spans is dense in $\mathcal S(\mathbb R)$.

For $H_{mn}(r)=\overline{\psi_m(\overline r)}\psi_n(r)$, Hermite
linearization and the Gaussian transform give a closed Fourier formula.  In
particular, $\widehat H_{mn}(0)=\delta_{mn}$.  Applying the arithmetic explicit
formula entrywise gives principal matrices
\[
 W_N=(\mathcal W[H_{mn}])_{m,n=0}^{N-1}.
\]
For every vector $a\in\mathbb C^N$,
\[
 a^*W_Na=\mathcal W\!\left[\left|\sum_{n<N}a_n\psi_n\right|^2\right].
\]
Thus positivity of all finite sections is exactly positivity on the algebraic
Hermite core.  Continuity of the fully regularized form would extend this to
its Schwartz closure.  The construction therefore converts the vague dense
family requirement into a concrete countable sequence of principal-minor
conditions.  It does not prove those conditions uniformly in $N$, does not yet
remove arithmetic cutoffs, and does not prove that the null space forces native
PhaseNav closure.
