This final synthesis records the state of the programme after integration of SOH-G024 into the repository mainline. Repository integration and mathematical canonization are deliberately distinguished: the canonical numbered theorem sequence remains audited through SOH-G023, while G024 is an integrated research line with exact results, an explicit computer-assisted dependency, and an open higher-order frontier.

\section*{Results that are closed}
The monograph establishes the half-axis fixed geometry, the binary complementarity lemmas, the exact centred quotient $\xi(1/2+z)=F(z^2)$, the positive Riemann-kernel representation, strict log-concavity statements at their declared levels, PF$_2$ for the quotient coefficients, the complete negative-inversion zero-pairing no-go, the coefficient-majorant disk and canonical half-mass law, the G021 PF$_2$-to-PF$_3$ insufficiency counterexample, and the G022--G023 exact PF$_3$ normal forms.

The G024 research line additionally proves the first two non-trivial complete-monotonicity inequalities for
\[
H_y(q)=D_y(\sqrt q),\qquad 0<|y|<\frac12.
\]
Specifically,
\[
H_y'(q)<0,\qquad H_y''(q)>0\qquad(q\ge0).
\]
The first inequality is analytic. The second combines analytic estimates with SOH-G024-Q, the reproducible outward-interval certificate for
\[
L''''(t)<20L''(t),\qquad L=-\log K,
\]
on the declared compact interval, plus a separate analytic tail proof and an exact downstream Riccati argument.

\section*{The present direct frontier}
The third-order recurrence is exact:
\[
F_{m+1}=S_yF_m-F_m',
\qquad
F_m=(-1)^m\frac{H_y^{(m)}}{H_y}.
\]
For $m=3$ the unresolved sign is encoded by the bridge cumulant--covariance combination
\[
R''=\mathbb E[C_u^{(3)}]-3\operatorname{Cov}(A_u,B_u)+\mu_3(A_u),
\]
or, equivalently, by the logarithmic-growth condition for the already positive second-order margin
\[
\frac{uM_2'}{M_2}\le uN_y+3.
\]
No universal proof of this inequality is claimed.

The bridge moment hierarchy remains a useful exact concentration input:
\[
\mathbb E[r^{2n}]<\frac{(2n+1)!!}{34^n},
\qquad
\mathbb E[e^{\lambda r^2}]\le(1-\lambda/17)^{-3/2},
\quad0\le\lambda<17.
\]

\section*{Open problems}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Third complete-monotonicity inequality.} Prove
\[
-H_y'''(q)\ge0
\]
for every $q\ge0$ and every $0<|y|<1/2$.

\item \textbf{Higher-order complete monotonicity.} Establish or falsify
\[
(-1)^mH_y^{(m)}(q)\ge0\qquad(m\ge3).
\]
A proof for every order would invoke the Hausdorff--Bernstein--Widder representation and yield a positive Gaussian-mixture representation for $D_y$ \citep{schilling2010bernstein}.

\item \textbf{External Fourier positivity.} Prove directly, or via complete monotonicity,
\[
\widehat D_y(x)>0
\]
for every real $x$ and $0<|y|<1/2$. By the Dimitrov--Xu correlation criterion and Wiener's Tauberian theorem, this is the direct RH-equivalent frontier used in G024 \citep{dimitrovxu2016,wiener1932tauberian}.

\item \textbf{Quotient real-rootedness.} Prove SOH-G003: every zero of $F$ is real. This remains an exact RH-equivalent formulation.

\item \textbf{PF$_3$ and PF$_\infty$.} Close the actual quotient-coefficient inequalities rather than abstract structural surrogates. G021 proves that the current PF$_2$ plus half-mass package is insufficient; G022 and G023 isolate sharper local targets.

\item \textbf{Full admissible Weil positivity.} Close SOH-C005 without circular use of an RH-equivalent premise.
\end{enumerate}

\section*{Final proof boundary}
The monograph does not prove the Riemann Hypothesis. Its present contribution is a set of exact reductions, theorems, no-go results, coefficient structures, and a direct Jensen--Wiener route whose first two complete-monotonicity inequalities are closed globally. The unresolved mathematical boundary is explicit:
\[
\boxed{m\ge3\text{ complete monotonicity: OPEN}},
\qquad
\boxed{\widehat D_y>0\text{ on the full strip: OPEN}},
\]
\[
\boxed{\mathrm{PF}_3,\mathrm{PF}_\infty,\mathrm{SOH\!\!-G003},\mathrm{SOH\!\!-C005}:\ OPEN},
\qquad
\boxed{\mathrm{RH}:\ OPEN}.
\]
