This roadmap separates the mathematical objects, theorem classes, computational certificates, and still-open implications used throughout the monograph. It is a reading guide, not an additional proof claim. \textbf{Repository integration} means that a research line is present on the active mainline and publication build; it does not by itself constitute mathematical canonization or proof promotion.

\section*{How to read the programme}
The monograph contains several independent routes that meet at the same proof boundary. The quotient route rewrites the centred xi function as $F(z^2)$ and studies real-rootedness and P\'olya-frequency structure. The Weil route studies positivity of the full admissible Weil form. The negative-inversion route develops an exact conformal/projective geometry but ultimately proves a spectral no-go for zero-to-zero action. The direct G024 route uses the Riemann correlation kernel and a complete-monotonicity sufficient condition for the Dimitrov--Xu/Wiener criterion.

\section*{Conformal coordinate maps}
The two coordinate maps most frequently used later are exact holomorphic maps away from their poles/branch identifications. The projective coordinate
\[
\Omega(s)=\frac{s}{1-s}
\]
sends the critical line to the unit circle, while the centred square quotient
\[
z=s-\frac12,\qquad w=z^2
\]
sends the critical line to the non-positive real axis.

\section*{Recommended reading order}
For a classical analytic reading, use Chapters~1--3, 25--31, 43--55, Appendix~D, and the final synthesis. For the information--spinor development, read Chapters~4--15 before the later no-go and quotient results. For the computational proof boundary, read Chapters~29, 50--55 together with the generated receipts and the reproducibility appendix material. All readers should use Appendix~E for the symbol table because several coordinates are intentionally local to their respective reductions.
