\section{Conventions}

All logarithms are natural. Entropies are measured in nats. Equality of state rays is denoted informally by $\sim$ or by square brackets $[\Psi]$. Numerical tolerances are not used in theorem statements. ``Zero'' without qualification means a non-trivial zeta zero when the context is the critical strip.
