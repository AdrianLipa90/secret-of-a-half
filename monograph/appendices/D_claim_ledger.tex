\section{Promotion rule}

An open claim may be promoted only when its complete proof or reproducible construction is present, every dependency is listed, and no dependency is merely an equivalent formulation of RH left unproved. Numerical agreement cannot promote a claim to exact status.

\subsection*{PhaseNav--Weil Hermite ladder, reciprocal duality, and prime-tail control}
\begin{description}
\item[SOH-L013, exact.] Finite spans of the translated-scaled Hermite channels are dense in Schwartz space.
\item[SOH-L014, exact.] The Hermite kernel has the declared closed Fourier transform and $\widehat H_{mn}(0)=\delta_{mn}$.
\item[SOH-T004, conditional.] Positivity of every finite Hermite section plus continuity extends non-negativity to the dense core.
\item[SOH-N004, numerical.] The finite ladder through the declared basis size is cutoff-audited and PSD-tested.
\item[SOH-L015, exact.] Binary complement is conjugate to reciprocal inversion in projective odds coordinates.
\item[SOH-L016, exact.] The unique positive reciprocal fixed point corresponds to $p=1/2$.
\item[SOH-L017, exact.] The half is the Fisher--Rao geodesic midpoint of the two labelled pure states.
\item[SOH-H001, exploratory.] Defined zero and undefined bottom are interpreted as informational boundary labels.
\item[SOH-L018, exact.] The logarithmic prime tail is compactified by $z_{\rm t}=1/\log x$ to a finite interval with a flat endpoint.
\item[SOH-L019, exact.] Every fixed Hermite entry has the declared incomplete-gamma prime-tail majorant.
\item[SOH-L020, exact finite section.] Entrywise tail certificates give an operator-norm and eigenvalue enclosure for every fixed finite section.
\item[SOH-N005, numerical.] For $N\le6$, $Q=100000$, the maximum certified tail norm is $7.7172\times10^{-13}$.
\item[SOH-L021, exact asymptotic.] Every exponential adaptive schedule $Q_N=e^{cN}$ with $c>0$ forces the declared coarse tail envelope to zero.
\item[SOH-N006, numerical.] For $w=0.8$, $Q_0=100000$, $c=2$, and $N\le20$, the maximum sharp certificate is $3.280365246530569\times10^{-14}$ at $N=5$.
\end{description}

The adaptive theorem controls a diagonal sequence of basis sizes and cutoffs. It does not prove fixed-cutoff uniformity, positivity of all infinite-cutoff sections, or the null-space bridge.
