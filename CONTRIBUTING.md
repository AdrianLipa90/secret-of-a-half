# Contributing

## Integrity rules

1. Separate proved statements, conditional statements, numerical evidence and interpretation.
2. Record every substantive change in `CHANGELOG.md` and `logs/` without rewriting earlier history.
3. Do not introduce hidden fitted parameters.
4. Every computational result must include enough provenance to reproduce it.
5. Do not state or imply that the Riemann Hypothesis has been proved unless every logical dependency has been independently verified.
